# Annual review of application planning sources

Owner: Planning product maintainer, with an admissions adviser reviewing applicability. Last register review: **2026-10-11**. Next scheduled review: **before publishing 2028-entry verified dates**, and at once on an official correction, new calendar, changed institution requirement or time-zone rule. This register covers every manual date, preparation timeline and requirement in `services/planning/src/templates.ts`, `actionable-resources.ts` and `applicant-rules.ts`; student-entered dates are independently confirmed with the selected institution.

`verified` means the source specifies the date, cutoff and applicable procedure. `requires_confirmation` includes missing cutoffs and suggested preparation targets. Day-only dates stay in task text and do not become midnight deadlines. A country choice is an applicant fact; the 249 ISO choices do not imply every country uses Études en France. Local guidance and institution participation still need confirmation. The initial coverage choices use the [Ministry’s country list](https://www.enseignementsup-recherche.gouv.fr/fr/etudiants-etrangers-inscriptions-dans-l-enseignement-superieur-francais-46508), reviewed 2026-10-11. Some Campus France local lists differ; review additions/removals with the country office before changing coverage.

## Researched country limits

Local route/calendar research in this release is limited to **France, the United States and Morocco for 2027 entry**. Only Morocco connected Études en France candidature and explicitly confirmed USA connected Licence 2/3 or Master’s HDAP candidature (excluding architecture and art) have verified closing instants. USA confirmed DAP (public-university first year or architecture at every level) and France-resident dossier vert DAP have verified closing days but no verified cutoff time. Parcoursup, Mon Master, institution-specific routes, every other country and every later cycle have no verified deadline in the new country-aware family. Their plans retain official guidance and tasks with deadline confirmation required. The 249-country chooser does not claim local calendar research for all countries.

## Review and release process

1. Inventory maintained content with searches for dates, years, deadline helpers, `sourceUrls`, `resourceSources`, `applicantSources`, card descriptions and checklist steps. Review both `templates.ts` and `actionable-resources.ts`; links can carry requirements even without a due date.
2. Read the primary authority's current page. Record entry cycle, residence and citizenship scope, qualification, study level, procedure, institution exclusions, local wall time and IANA zone. If the source lacks a cutoff or has conflicting year labels, leave the date unresolved. Check the local office; do not reuse another country's calendar.
3. For institution-specific requirements, retain the institution's official URL and applicable round. Distinguish suggestions from official submission dates. Refresh all linked workflow/documentation pages; inherited review dates above do not certify a new calendar.
4. Add a new immutable template release and rule revision. Never edit historical definitions in `legacyTemplates`, `milestoneTemplatesForCycle` or `actionableTemplatesForCycle`; seeding uses `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`. Changing a rule module for a future release also requires preserving the earlier rule implementation for historical re-evaluation.
5. Validate personalized preview and server-derived instantiation for unknown facts, dual citizenship, French/European qualifications, noncitizen aid eligibility, CSS participation, country isolation, USA confirmed HDAP vs architecture DAP at all levels vs art/unknown, rejected first-year HDAP, future unverified cycles, profile whitelist and edited board dates. Run affected Planning, Clients and Web checks.
6. Publish only after the source register and applicability tests agree. Existing boards keep their tasks and dates. Updates remain explicit through template review; boards that lack newly required facts need a new confirmed personalized plan. Provider acceptance, portal display and actual application submission are separate outcomes.

The current country catalog offers official guidance for every ISO residence choice, with locally researched France/USA/Morocco links. The initial rule release is `2026-10-11.1`; new country-aware Campus France templates use `campus-france-<entry year>` v1; Common App uses v4 for 2027 and v3 for later cycles. Earlier Common App v1–v3 and Morocco family versions retain their original semantics. Future cycles contain useful tasks with dates requiring confirmation.
